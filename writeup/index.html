Technical write-up

A complete account of the search, its calibration and its results, in eight chapters. Each stands on its own; together they document every step from raw TESS data to the four candidates.

Elsewhere

The chapters are also Markdown files in the repository: docs/. Per-candidate dossiers with every number and figure: results/candidates/. A generated summary of every number: REPORT.md. A short note in AAS Research Note format: paper/rnaas_note.tex.
